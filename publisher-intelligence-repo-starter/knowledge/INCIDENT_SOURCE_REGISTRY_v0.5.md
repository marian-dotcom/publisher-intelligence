## Source precedence
1. Current official platform status/postmortem
2. Primary technical issue tracker
3. Official publisher/vendor case study

## Provenance rules
- Preserve URL, source type and evidence tier.
- Never invent a missing root cause or recovery.
- Separate certainty that an event happened from certainty it caused a publisher symptom.
- External Google/CDN/vendor incidents are context until matched to local evidence.
- Community threads teach patterns/counterexamples; they do not define canonical behavior.
- Counts measure corpus coverage, not real-world cause frequency.
